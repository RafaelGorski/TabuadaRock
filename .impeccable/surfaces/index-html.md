## Scope and mode

The whole TabuadaRock game, served from `index.html`: attract mode, menus, ladder, training, fights, results, the progress board, and the ending. Mode is **Experience**, with Operate-level clarity for the question and answer loop.

## Audience, job, task

An 11-year-old learning the times tables from scratch, playing at home on a laptop with keyboard and mouse. Their job is to finish the tables of 2 through 10. Each table runs as one match:

- **Treino:** skip-count orbs teach the table.
- **Round 1:** facts in order.
- **Round 2:** facts shuffled.
- **Final round:** shuffled facts plus review from earlier tables, with a gentle clock.

A champion fight then mixes every table. Answers are typed. The mouse uses an on-screen keypad.

## Constraints

- All text is in pt-BR.
- Creatures are original, never Pokémon.
- No timer while learning. The clock appears only in final rounds and the champion fight.
- It must never feel like homework or a repetitive drill.

## Chosen direction

**Fliperama de Luta:** an arcade fighting game in 3D. A correct answer is the hit and the product is the damage number. Supporting pieces:

- Training-room grid floor for teaching.
- ∞ round clock that turns into a real countdown in final rounds.
- Combo counter, super meter, and K.O.
- A ladder of guardians that join your team.

## Memorable moments

- The full orb wall for the table fires at the training dummy once all ten facts are built.
- The attract-mode cold open lands 6 × 7 = 42 as a hit before the title slams in.
